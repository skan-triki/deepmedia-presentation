The newsroom's daily reality

Many tools. Multiple back-and-forths. One piece of content.

Newsrooms juggle dozens of disconnected subscriptions that forget your voice, waste time and push up costs.

Stacked licenses

5 to 10 separate subscriptions that do not communicate with each other.

AI without identity

Generic assistants that ignore your charter and tone.

Lost time

Hours of reformatting, copy-pasting, and reconciling versions.

Hidden costs

Licenses, freelancers, and corrections that pile up unchecked.

Inconsistent tone

Every tool starts from scratch and forgets your values and your voice.

Weakened credibility

The audience no longer distinguishes your voice from generic content.
